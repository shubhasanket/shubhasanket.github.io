### About me <br>

I am a second-year Master's student in the **M2 Mathématiques, Vision, Apprentissage (MVA)** program at **ENS Paris-Saclay (Université Paris-Saclay)**, supported by the **France Excellence Charpak Master Scholarship**. My work lies at the interface of **applied mathematics, AI, and machine learning**, with a strong interest in mathematically grounded methods for reliable prediction, uncertainty quantification, and large-scale data-driven modelling. I completed the first year of my Master's in Applied Mathematics at **ENSIMAG - Université Grenoble Alpes**, where I received the **Graduate School@UGA Excellence Scholarship** and ranked **first** in my cohort. Before that, I obtained a B.Sc. in Mathematics and Physics from the **Sri Aurobindo International Centre of Education** in Puducherry, India.

I am currently a research intern at the **[Computational and Applied Mathematics Laboratory (CAMLab)](https://camlab.ethz.ch/), Seminar for Applied Mathematics, D-MATH, ETH Zürich**, under the supervision of **[Prof. Siddhartha Mishra](https://camlab.ethz.ch/the-group/group-head.html)**, and supported by the **DataIA Paris-Saclay Institute International Mobility Scholarship**. My research focuses on **diffusion-based certificates of trust for AI prediction models**, with a particular emphasis on **large foundation models** and scientific AI systems trained on massive datasets, including several-petabyte-scale data. Previously, I was a research intern in the **AIRSEA** team at **Laboratoire Jean Kuntzmann (CNRS)** in Grenoble, where I worked on statistical inference methods for calibrating diffusion terms in stochastic differential equations.

These experiences have strengthened my interest in **applied amthematics, large-scale computing, mathematical machine learning, and quantitative research**. I am particularly interested in applying rigorous mathematical, computational and machine learning-based methods for reliable prediction in complex data-driven and data-intensive systems.
