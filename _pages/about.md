### About me <br>

I am a second-year Master's student in Applied Mathematics in the Mathématiques, Vision, Apprentissage (M2 MVA) program at École Normale Supérieure Paris-Saclay (Université Paris-Saclay)**, and a recipient of the France Excellence Charpak Master Scholarship. I completed the first year of my Master's in Applied Mathematics at ENSIMAG - Université Grenoble Alpes, where I received the Graduate School@UGA Excellence Scholarship and ranked first in my cohort. Before that, I graduated with a B.Sc. in Mathematics and Physics from the Sri Aurobindo International Centre of Education in Puducherry, India.

I am currently a research intern at the [Computational and Applied Mathematics Laboratory (CAMLab)](https://camlab.ethz.ch/), Seminar for Applied Mathematics, D-MATH, ETH Zürich, under the supervision of [Prof. Siddhartha Mishra](https://camlab.ethz.ch/the-group/group-head.html), for which I received the DataIA Paris-Saclay Institute International Mobility Scholarship. My research focuses on diffusion-based certificates of trust for AI prediction model, with a particular emphasis on foundation models and scientific AI systems trained on massive datasets, including several-petabyte-scale data. Previously, I was a research intern in the AIRSEA team at [Laboratoire Jean Kuntzmann (CNRS)](https://www-ljk.imag.fr/?lang=en) in Grenoble, where I worked on statistical inference methods for calibrating diffusion terms in stochastic differential equations. My work lies at the interface of applied mathematics, AI, and machine learning, with a strong interest in mathematically grounded methods for reliable prediction, uncertainty quantification, and large-scale data-driven modelling.
