### About me <br>

I am a final-year Master's student in Applied Mathematics, Machine Learning and Quantitative Finance in the M2 Probabilités et Finance (ex DEA El Karoui) at Sorbonne Université and École Polytechnique. Before this, I completed the M2 Mathématiques, Vision, Apprentissage (MVA), a research Master's in Applied Mathematics and Machine Learning, at École Normale Supérieure Paris-Saclay (Université Paris-Saclay), for which I was awarded the France Excellence Charpak Master Scholarship. I completed the first year of my Master's in Applied Mathematics at ENSIMAG - Université Grenoble Alpes, where I received the Graduate School@UGA Excellence Scholarship and ranked first in my cohort. Before that, I graduated with a B.Sc. in Mathematics and Physics from the Sri Aurobindo International Centre of Education in Pondicherry, India, and was awarded the Best Student Prize.

I am currently a student researcher at the [Computational and Applied Mathematics Laboratory (CAMLab)](https://camlab.ethz.ch/), Seminar for Applied Mathematics, D-MATH, ETH Zürich, working with [Prof. Siddhartha Mishra](https://camlab.ethz.ch/the-group/group-head.html). This role follows my research internship at CAMLab from April to September 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Mishra, for which I received the DataIA Paris-Saclay Institute International Mobility Scholarship. My research develops diffusion-based certificates of trust for AI prediction models, which estimate prediction error without access to ground truth, and I am now extending this framework to Aurora, a large-scale weather foundation model; a manuscript is in preparation. Previously, I was a research intern in the AIRSEA team at [Laboratoire Jean Kuntzmann (CNRS)](https://www-ljk.imag.fr/?lang=en) in Grenoble, where I worked on statistical inference methods for calibrating diffusion terms in stochastic differential equations. My work lies at the interface of applied mathematics, AI, and machine learning, with a strong interest in mathematically grounded methods for reliable prediction, uncertainty quantification, and large-scale data-driven modelling and decision making.
